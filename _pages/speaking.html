Long

David Andersson is a security engineering leader with more than twenty years of experience, from a Swedish government agency to high-growth SaaS. He has built security functions from the ground up and works across application security, vulnerability management, incident response and governance. He holds CISSP and CISM certifications and has spoken at OWASP Global AppSec EU 2025 and GrafanaCON 2026.

His background spans technical security consulting, public sector protective security and CISO work, and security engineering leadership at software companies. He approaches security as a service function, not a gatekeeper, and keeps technical judgement close to the work. He represented Sweden in SIS TK 318, the standardisation committee for the ISO 27000 family.
